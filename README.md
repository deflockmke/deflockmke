# DeFlock MKE

Our mission is to **protect our right to privacy**, to **fight back against the surveillance state**, and to **make our communities safe** for us to live in.

This repository contains content for a static site to facilitate this mission.

## Our Goals ##
1. **End existing ALPR contracts.**

This is self-explanatory. Many of our communities currently have contracts with Flock or another ALPR provider to spy on us. The mission of protecting our privacy starts with getting rid of whatever contracts are currently in place.

2. **Pass binding measures to prevent future adoption of ALPR technologies.**

Simply cancelling our contracts isn’t enough. Oftentimes, communities that cancel their contracts with Flock just adopt another ALPR provider, like Axon, to continue to surveillance. We need to make sure this kind of surveillance stays out of our communities for good.

3. **Fully account for the data already collected.**

One of the biggest dangers of ALPRs is that the information they collect gets stored in a centralized database. When contracts are cancelled, that data doesn’t automatically get deleted. Whether it still exists in the hands of our police and government officials, or if it still exists in the privately owned servers of ALPR providers like Flock, that data continues to make us vulnerable as long as it exists. We need to make sure that the information collected by mass warrantless surveillance is not used to continue to make predictive policing decisions, that it is not sitting on a database somewhere to be exploited by hackers or foreign adversaries, and that it is not sold unscrupulously to the highest bidder!
